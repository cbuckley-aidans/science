Key Concepts

Pure Substances vs Mixtures

Definition

Pure Substance

Contains only one type of particle. All particles are identical. Examples: diamond, oxygen gas, distilled water. Many substances that look uniform are actually mixtures.

Definition

Mixture

Contains two or more substances combined but not chemically joined. All mixtures can be separated into their components using physical methods that rely on differences in properties.
