Key Concepts

Two Big Ideas

Topic 1

Classification

The process of grouping organisms with similar features. The Linnaean system organises all living things into seven levels: Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, and Species.

Topic 2

Food Chains & Webs

Models that show how energy and matter flow between organisms in an ecosystem. A food chain shows one sequence; a food web joins many chains together.
