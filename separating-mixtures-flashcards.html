Key Concepts

What Are We Separating?

Definition

Soluble Substance

A substance that dissolves in a solvent (such as water) to form a solution. Once dissolved, the substance cannot be seen. Example: salt dissolved in water.

Definition

Insoluble Substance

A substance that does not dissolve in a solvent. It remains as a visible solid that can be seen in the liquid. Example: sand mixed with water.
